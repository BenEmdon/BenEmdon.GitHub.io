$ how i make pixel art

It starts as characters in a grid. Each letter is a colour, and the picture is just the map, drawn one whole cell at a time.

Now a turn for you. Pick a colour, tap the cells, stamp a tree. Nothing here drifts or fades: a cell is on, or it is not.

That is the whole recipe. Steal it.
